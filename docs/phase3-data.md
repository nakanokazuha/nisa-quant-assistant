# Phase 3 data sources and coverage

Phase 3 is read-only and cache-first. It stores normalized JSON snapshots under the ignored local `data/phase3/` directory. A snapshot records actual first/last observation, row count, gaps, retrieval time, source, adjusted-close/dividend/split availability, and a coverage limitation for every ticker.

## Market data

The live adapter uses the public Yahoo Finance chart endpoint as an explicitly unofficial/reference source. Requests are bounded to the configured date range, use retry/backoff only for transient HTTP statuses, and never accept credentials. Yahoo chart `adjclose`, dividend, and split events are retained when returned; unavailable fields remain explicit. The default request target is approximately 30 years, but the report uses the actual provider-returned range and never claims that every ticker has 30 years.

Every market-bar boundary enforces finite, strictly positive OHLC values, the high/low relationships, finite non-negative volume, one observation per UTC-normalized date, a well-formed non-empty `retrieved_at`, and non-empty source and citation/evidence identifiers. Retrieval must not precede the observation date. Zero volume is accepted as a valid observation; dollar volume is `0.0` and volume trend is the deterministic neutral value `0.0` when the rolling volume mean is zero. Invalid bars and duplicate normalized dates are omitted or rejected fail-closed with `invalid_observation` evidence; they cannot create a cache or panel/report rows.

The benchmark default is `^GSPC`, the S&P 500 price index. It is not silently substituted with an ETF or a total-return index. The live producer retrieves the current constituent table from [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_S%26P_500_companies), records the source URL and retrieval time, normalizes provider punctuation at the Yahoo boundary, and rejects a small-universe fallback. This is current-survivor membership only: it is not historical constituent membership and cannot support historical alpha or benchmark-relative performance claims.

Universe membership uses finite `active`/`inactive`, `point_in_time`/`current_snapshot_only`, and explicit survivorship-bias statuses. A row is point-in-time evidence only when both effective dates are canonical and ordered and its source/provenance fields are present; current-only, unclaimed, incomplete, or ambiguous membership is descriptive and suppresses performance, alpha, and benchmark-relative claims.

Provider partial failures remain explicit per ticker and are considered by the live coverage policy. A partial snapshot may be returned for that in-memory decision, but it is never written as a history cache and partial cache artifacts are not replayable.

Forward targets preserve separate horizons: the 1-month asset, benchmark, and excess fields use each decision date to the next monthly decision date as a disjoint interval; 3-month, 6-month, and 12-month labels use 90, 180, and 365 calendar-day endpoints and record availability explicitly. The 3-month excess target is the production default for model fitting and prediction ranking; a selected 6-month or 12-month fit purges labels using that horizon's own observability. No rank-IC metric is calculated. Monthly portfolio and benchmark compounding use only the disjoint 1-month price-return fields.

## SEC evidence

The read-only adapter first validates ticker→CIK through the official [`company_tickers.json`](https://www.sec.gov/files/company_tickers.json) mapping, then reads only the canonical `https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK##########.json` Company Facts endpoint with a locally supplied non-secret contact string. SEC retrieval is optional: disabled, unavailable, and zero-usable-fact outcomes remain explicit in the manifest and do not invalidate valid market-only predictions. Facts retain concept, unit, period end, filing form, accession, citation, and filing availability time. Missing or invalid filing dates are unavailable, never replaced by period end. Submissions, filings beyond Company Facts, and news are unavailable/deferred unless separately wired; no SEC key is used and no raw response is logged.

SEC is optional and coverage is explicit per ticker. The live producer currently performs only a bounded probe (the first selected ticker when `--sec-contact` is supplied), so a successful probe does not represent SEC coverage for every constituent. Unprobed tickers are recorded as such in the manifest; absent facts remain `null` in features and are handled only by the documented training-only missing-feature policy.

Panel eligibility is stricter than feature imputation: a universe member must have at least one finite close observation on or before the decision date. A ticker with zero bars, a provider failure, or no usable pre-decision observation is recorded in coverage/failure and panel-exclusion manifest fields and is omitted from both the training panel and current prediction ranking. Optional SEC features may still be imputed from training-derived values; market history is never imputed.

Current predictions use a deterministic monthly freshness policy: the latest usable market observation for a monthly decision row may be at most 31 calendar days before `as_of` (`monthly_decision_date_31_calendar_day_allowance_v1`). This accommodates a monthly row whose decision date is the first of the month while retaining a finite one-month bound against stale history. The panel records the latest usable market observation date; the producer manifest and each prediction record the policy, threshold, as-of date, observation date, and measured age. Stale, malformed, future-dated, or unavailable series are excluded from normal current predictions and receive no synthesized score.

## Cache and replay

The `fetch_history_snapshot` Python API writes a content-addressed cache snapshot, and repeating the same request replays a valid local snapshot when present. Its cache request contract binds the sorted asset tickers, benchmark ticker and disjoint role, inclusive request dates and provider epochs, daily interval, dividend/split events, `price_return` basis, and the versioned request schema. Cache filenames, response identity, array lengths/types, date bounds, and content hashes are validated; writes are atomic, and partial snapshots are never saved or used. `phase3-refresh` is replay-only unless `--live` is explicit. If a public provider is unavailable, the producer emits a structured unavailable manifest/report rather than fabricating partial history.

Public endpoint terms, availability, rate limits, and data quality can change. The Yahoo source is not presented as an official exchange feed, and users should review its current terms before relying on generated research.
